# Droplet assembly notes

1. Solder the TMC2208 carrier and the RP2040 module onto the driver board.
2. Press the magnetic home switch into the bracket slot.
3. Mount the pump head with four M3 screws; do not overtighten the tubing barbs.
4. Flash the firmware and run the two-point calibration.
5. Fit the reservoir bracket and check that the tubing does not rub the lid.

Revision B boards are marked `DRV-B` next to the USB connector.
